Who's behind this site

Who runs it, where the numbers come from, how to report an error and how the site is kept going.

Who's behind it

Hi, I'm Francesco. I have worked in human resources and training for more than 20 years. Every day I deal with cases involving fixed-term contracts, benefits and social security formalities.

This is not professional advice and it does not replace a patronato (an office that helps with INPS applications) or an accountant. It turns rules I see applied every day into practical tools: rules that almost no online calculator handles when a working life is made up of many short jobs.

How the calculators are built

Every parameter is taken from the text of the law or of the circular that sets it, not from second-hand summaries. At the bottom of each page you will find the exact articles and the date they were checked.

This matters more than it seems: while building the site I found authoritative sources publishing the wrong formula, applying to employees the tax credit (detrazione) meant for pensioners. Anyone who copies from those sources spreads the error.

The figures are updated every January, when the Budget Law and the INPS circular with the revalued amounts come out.

If a result doesn't add up

If a figure does not match what you actually received from INPS or read on your payslip, that is valuable information. Reports are the only way to correct the borderline cases that the written rule does not let you foresee: write to me from the contact page (in Italian), giving the details you entered and the figure you expected.

How the site is kept going

Today the site has no advertising, uses no cookies and has no statistics tools: the calculations happen in your browser and are not sent anywhere. The details are on the privacy page (in Italian).

Keeping a site running costs time and some money, so I do not rule out that advertising may appear in the future. If that happens, it will be written here and on the privacy page, with the date, and you will be asked for consent before any cookie is installed. What will not change is the rest: no form to fill in to see a result, no registration, no content hidden behind a payment.
