How much NASpI you're entitled to

NASpI is the unemployment benefit paid by INPS in Italy. Short contracts, agency work, weeks already paid out: each one changes the result. Enter them all here and see the payment month by month.

  • INPS Circular 4/2026
  • Rules checked on 13 November 2026
  • The numbers you enter stay in your browser
1Your pay

Average pay subject to social security contributions (imponibile previdenziale) over the last 4 years, not just your last payslip.

Add up the imponibile previdenziale on your CU forms (Certificazione Unica) or on your INPS contribution statement.

2Your contributions

Add up all your contracts, even short ones and with different agencies. The theoretical maximum is 208.

Weeks of contributions already used for a previous NASpI: twice the number of weeks of NASpI you received. Count only those that fall within the last 4 years. Leave 0 if this doesn't apply to you.

From age 55, the 3% cut starts in month 8 instead of month 6.

Frequently asked questions

I had short contracts with different agencies. How do I count the weeks?
You add them all up, whatever the employer or agency. What counts are the weeks with unemployment insurance contributions in the four years before your job ended, even if they are split across ten contracts. The exact figure is on your INPS contribution statement (estratto conto contributivo).
I already received NASpI in the last four years. Can I get it again?
Yes, if you have at least thirteen weeks of contributions in the four years before your new job ended and you lost that job involuntarily. The duration changes, though: weeks of contributions already used for a previous NASpI are not counted a second time, so the new benefit is shorter. The weeks used are twice the number of weeks of NASpI you received; only those that still fall within the last four years are taken off.
How long does NASpI last if I only worked a few months?
Half of the weeks of contributions. Thirteen weeks of work give six and a half weeks of benefit, about a month and a half: the last payment is reduced in proportion, it does not disappear. The absolute maximum is 104 weeks, 24 months.
How does the monthly cut (décalage) work?
The payment goes down by 3% every month from the sixth month, or from the eighth if you were 55 or older when you applied. The cut adds up, because each month it applies to the previous month's amount.
When do I have to apply?
Within 68 days of the end of your contract, and only after it has ended. You apply online on the INPS website, also through a patronato (an office that helps with INPS applications). If you apply by the eighth day after the job ends, NASpI starts from the eighth day; if you apply later, from the day after your application.
I'm a foreigner. Am I entitled to NASpI?
Yes, with the same requirements as Italians: you lost your job involuntarily and you have at least thirteen weeks of contributions in the last four years. If you resigned from a permanent job in the twelve months before, you need thirteen weeks of contributions after that resignation. Citizenship does not matter. INPS does exclude: non-EU workers with a residence permit for seasonal work (permesso di soggiorno per lavoro stagionale); farm workers (operai agricoli), who have a different benefit (except permanent workers of some farm cooperatives); permanent public-sector employees; people who already qualify for an old-age or early retirement pension; and people who receive the ordinary invalidity allowance (assegno ordinario di invalidità) and do not choose NASpI instead.
If I go back to my country, do I keep receiving NASpI?
It depends on how long you stay away. You can go abroad, in Europe or outside it, without explaining why: this is stated in INPS Circular 177/2017. But NASpI continues only as long as you meet your obligations towards the Job Centre (Centro per l'impiego) in Italy: if it calls you in and you do not attend, or you do not take part in the agreed activities, without a valid reason, your NASpI is first reduced and then stopped. Being abroad is not a valid reason: people who go back to their country for a long time, or for good, risk losing it. If you start a new job abroad, NASpI is suspended. If you go to look for work in another European country there is a special procedure: see the next question.
Can I look for work in another European country without losing NASpI?
Yes, for a maximum of three months from your departure, in the countries of the European Union and in Iceland, Liechtenstein, Norway and Switzerland. You must have been registered with the Job Centre (Centro per l'impiego) for at least one day. Before leaving, tell the Job Centre you are no longer available and cancel your registration, then ask the INPS office for documents U2 and U1, in person and by appointment. Within seven days of leaving, register with the employment office of the country you go to and hand in the U2: if you register later, NASpI is paid only from the day you register, and the three months are not extended. Until that office informs INPS, payments are suspended. During these three months the Italian obligations do not apply, but the rules and checks of that country's employment office do. From the fourth month, to keep receiving NASpI, you must register again with a Job Centre in Italy and meet its obligations. Two warnings. If you leave to look for work without telling the Job Centre, INPS recovers all the NASpI paid from the day you left. And if you are not a citizen of one of these countries, INPS applies this procedure only if you are resident in an EU country and have been insured in at least two member states: ask INPS before you leave.
I'm a non-EU citizen. If I lose my job, do I lose my residence permit?
No. Losing your job is not a reason to revoke your residence permit (permesso di soggiorno), for you or for your family members who are legally resident. If you have a permit for employment (lavoro subordinato), you can declare to the Job Centre (Centro per l'impiego) that you are available for work and stay in Italy to look for a job for the time left on your permit and in any case for at least one year, or for the whole duration of your NASpI if that is longer. The one-year minimum does not apply to seasonal work permits. If your permit expires while you are still unemployed, apply to renew it as a job-seeking permit (permesso per attesa occupazione). After this period, to renew it again you need a minimum income, which can also be that of family members who live with you. The rule is article 22, paragraph 11, of Legislative Decree 286/1998.

How it is calculated

✓ Figures and rules checked on 13 November 2026.

Amount. 75% of your average monthly pay up to €1,456.72, plus 25% of any part above that. The maximum is €1,584.70 gross a month.

Duration. INPS counts weeks, not whole months: half of the weeks of contributions in the last four years, excluding those already used for a previous NASpI (twice the weeks of NASpI received, counting only those that fall within the four years), up to a maximum of 104. When a fraction is left over, the last payment is reduced in proportion instead of being dropped.

Monthly cut (décalage). 3% a month from the sixth month, from the eighth for people aged 55 or over when they apply. The cut adds up.

Sources. INPS Circular no. 4 of 28 January 2026 for the threshold and the maximum. Articles 3, 4, 5 and 6 of Legislative Decree 22/2015 as currently in force for requirements, monthly cut, duration and application; the requirement of thirty days of actual work has not applied since 2022 (art. 3, paragraph 1-bis); the rule on resignations has applied since 2025 (art. 3, paragraph 1, letter c-bis). INPS Circulars 94/2015 and 142/2015 for weeks already used. Law 199/2025 did not change ordinary NASpI. For exclusions, the INPS page NASpI: indennità mensile di disoccupazione. For people who go abroad, the same page, INPS Circular 177/2017 and the INPS page Disoccupazione per chi si sposta nei Paesi UE, SEE e in Svizzera. For residence permits, article 22, paragraph 11, of Legislative Decree 286/1998.

What this tool does not do. It does not handle vertical part-time work (part time verticale) or the rule on voluntary resignation from a permanent job in the previous twelve months. It does not apply to people excluded from NASpI: the list is in the questions above. Amounts are gross: INPS withholds income tax (IRPEF) at source. It is an estimate, not a decision: only INPS's decision is binding.
